**Past AUV Platforms**

AquaPack has had a number of AUV platforms over the years. As of 2026, our current platform is SWIX. This page features photos of our past platforms! More information can be found on [our website](https://aquapackrobotics.org). If you have interest in a particular sub, look for a [Technical Design Report](https://aquapackrobotics.org/tdrs/) of that Sub on our website.

## Recent Robots
### Seawolf VIII

![SeaWolf VIII autonomous underwater vehicle](/mechanical/quickstart/seawolf-viii.jpg)
[SeaWolf VIII](https://aquapackrobotics.org/seawolves/viii/) was created with the goal of improving platform stability and consistency to enable future development work. This iteration drew from the lessons learned from previous iterations, focusing on simplicity and maintainability throughout the design.

SWVIII was used from RoboSub 2020-2025, and was retired because its fischer connectors were beginning to corrode.

### Seawolf VII (RoboSub 2017 and 2019)

![SeaWolf VII autonomous underwater vehicle](/mechanical/quickstart/seawolf-vii.jpg)
SWVII is a testament to the importance of proper interdisciplinary collaboration. It was constructed without a mechanical team, and experienced structural failures due to its use of structural acrylic (don’t use acrylic for structural stuff… it breaks). SWVII is the last robot for which the club currently has a large amount of information, but Technical Design Reports for our AUVs can be found back to SWII.
