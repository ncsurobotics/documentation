## Summary

![AquaPack Robotics Mechanical Quickstart Guide cover illustration](/mechanical/quickstart/quickstart-cover.png)

So, you want to build an underwater robot? Great! As you’ve probably already realized,
working underwater comes with a number of challenges not faced on land. This
document will point you in the right direction and give you some resources to get you
started!

This document was started by the Mechanical team of Spring 2026 and is aimed at
providing both general engineering design guidance and underwater design guidance.
Information is divided into 3 main sections; AquaPack Cultural, which discusses club
history and practices; General Mechanical Design, which covers topics such as 3D
printing, CAD modeling, and documentation expectations; and Underwater Design, which
covers some of the trickier aspects of designing underwater robots.

This document has a heavy mechanical bend to it, and is focused on mechanical
design. For a broader look at RoboSub, including a high level look at general software
and electrical systems, check out Team Inspiration’s excellent RoboSub 101 guide at
their website, [RoboSub 101](https://www.inspirationrobosub.com/robosub101).
