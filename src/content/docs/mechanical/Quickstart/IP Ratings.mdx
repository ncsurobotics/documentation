## What is an IP Rating?

Ingress Protection (IP) Ratings are the degree to which a device is waterproof/dustproof. This guide will teach you how to read and understand the different types of ratings you will see.

## Why do we care?

It is an underwater robot (duh). Electricity and water do not play well together. Every part that is used in the housing or sealing of on board electronics must be of a certain IP Rating to ensure that the electronics will be safe.

## How do we read it?

[From IEC](https://www.iec.ch/ip-ratings): The first numeral refers to the protection against solid objects and is rated on a scale from 0 (no protection) to 6 (no ingress of dust). The second numeral rates the enclosure’s protection against liquids and uses a scale from 0 (no protection) to 9 (high-pressure hot water from different angles).

## AquaPack Specifications

Our robot is underwater for prolonged periods of time. As such, we typically want an IP of IP68 (or better). We DO check waterproofing prior to each pool test, however, we DO NOT check during testing. Do not rely on someone catching waterproofing errors, please ensure that the robot is properly waterproofed.

Note that IP 68 components are only technically rated for 1 meter depth and 30 minutes of submersion. Many IP 68 components can handle longer and deeper submersion, but it is always advised to check manufacturer specs to see if manufacturers provide specific information on their testing methodologies.
