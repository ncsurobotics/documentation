*Written by Alexandria Epley*

**In Conclusion…**

So, that's a wrap on Version 1.0 of the AquaPack Robotics Mechanical Quick Start Guide. I hope it was helpful! AquaPack has been a huge part of my life for the last two years, and I spent a lot of time googling how to do things other people in the club had done years ago. I hope this document alleviates some of that, though I'm sure there's a ton of things that we missed when writing it (including, in this section, some typos… I didn't let Alexis proofread the conclusion).

If you read through this entire document, and didn't just jump to the conclusion to see how incredibly witty it was, then you now have a baseline understanding of what I believe are the fundamentals of mechanical design for underwater robots. And, as you build on that knowledge base, I hope you and the mechanical team at the time you are reading this will work together to expand this quickstart guide into a V 2.0 and beyond (Someone qualified needs to rewrite the machining section, for starters).

Happy CADing, and Go Pack!

- Alexandria Epley
